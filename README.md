
# Underlying Dimensions and Determinants of Technological Drought in Relatively Uplifted Regions of Bangladesh

## Overview

This repository documents the research published in *Water Scarcity and Drought* (2026), investigating the underlying dimensions and determinants of technological drought in the Madhupur Tract of Bangladesh.

The study examines farmers' perceptions, irrigation practices, technological constraints, and socioeconomic factors associated with technological drought using a mixed-methods research approach.

## Study Area

The research focuses on the Madhupur Tract of Bangladesh, covering parts of Tangail, Mymensingh, Jamalpur, and Gazipur districts.

## Research Design and Methods

- Mixed-methods research approach
- Structured questionnaire survey
- 459 valid farmer responses
- 55 survey variables
- Stratified random sampling
- Data screening and standardization
- Principal Component Analysis (PCA)
- KMO and Bartlett's tests
- Kaiser criterion, scree plot, and parallel analysis
- Varimax rotation with Kaiser normalization

Ten principal components were identified to represent the underlying dimensions of technological drought.
