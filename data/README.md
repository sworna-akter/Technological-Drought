
# Data

This folder documents the data used in the research project **"Underlying Dimensions and Determinants of Technological Drought in Relatively Uplifted Regions of Bangladesh."**

The study investigates farmers' experiences, perceptions, agricultural practices, and technological constraints associated with technological drought in the Madhupur Tract of Bangladesh.

## Study Area

The study covers the Madhupur Tract region, including four districts:

- Tangail
- Mymensingh
- Jamalpur
- Gazipur

The study area covers approximately 4,244 km².

## Data Sources

The research primarily used primary data collected through:

- Structured household and farmer surveys conducted using the Qualtrics online survey platform.
- Field interviews with farmers and individuals involved in agricultural activities.
- Focus group discussions and consultations with local agricultural officers, extension workers, and community leaders.

Secondary information was also consulted from relevant official reports, journal articles, and local administrative statistics.

## Survey Dataset

A total of 471 respondents were interviewed. Following data cleaning and the removal of incomplete or erroneous records, 459 valid responses were retained for analysis.

The questionnaire incorporated 55 socioeconomic, agronomic, technological, and perception-based variables/statements related to technological drought.

The survey covered several themes, including:

- Irrigation facilities and water availability
- Energy and fuel availability
- Agricultural technology and irrigation practices
- Socioeconomic conditions of farming households
- Farmers' perceptions of drought occurrence and impacts
- Access to agricultural information and institutional support
- Drought preparedness and adaptation practices
- Environmental and policy-related constraints

## Data Availability and Access

The respondent-level dataset is not included in this public repository. According to the published article, the dataset is available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request.

Researchers interested in accessing the original survey dataset should contact the corresponding author and follow the applicable data-access and ethical requirements.

NOTE: No identifiable respondent information or confidential survey records should be uploaded to this repository without appropriate authorization.
